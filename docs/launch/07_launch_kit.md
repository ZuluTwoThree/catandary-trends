## 7.1 Product one-pager

**Catandary — evidence-based, local-first trend & foresight intelligence.**

**The problem.** By the time a trend is a headline, it's already late. The signal was in
the research years earlier, then the patents, then the funding. Most trend tools only watch
the market — the last stop — and hand you a number with no source.

**What it is.** A platform that reads the whole innovation chain — **science → patents →
funding → market** — and turns over a million signals into trend clusters, momentum and
technology-improvement rates, with every number traceable to its primary source.

**How it works.** Collect (243 legal primary sources) → Classify (local models; 8 verticals
× 6 PESTEL × mega/macro/micro) → Cluster (bottom-up, ranked by normalized momentum) →
Attribute (every trend linked to its source; fabrications held back).

**What's inside (verified).** 1M+ analysed signals · 60,000+ curated articles · 221 live
trend clusters · a Technology Improvement Rate on the peer-reviewed SPNP method (Singh,
Triulzi & Magee 2021) over a ~109M-edge patent-citation graph · a trend radar with
lead-time rings · semantic search with a live analytics sidebar.

**Who it's for.** Corporate innovation & foresight teams · agencies & consultants ·
prosumer analysts.

**Why it's different.** The whole chain (not just market news) · evidence on every number ·
honest by construction (withholds what it can't prove; 23/24 known trends recovered, 6/6
momentum calls correct) · local models → a self-service price between free keyword tools and
€50k+ enterprise suites.

**Pricing.** Free · Starter €99 · Pro €499 · Super Pro+ €799 /mo · Hypercare €1,499/day
(sales-led). **CTA.** Get the free weekly briefing → catandary.de.

## 7.4 Sales & demo

### 5-minute demo script
1. **Hook (30s).** "Most tools tell you a trend after it's arrived. Watch me show you one
   *before* it does." Open the landing hero — the lead-time instrument.
2. **The chain (60s).** Methodology page: the four tiers, live corpus counter, "no black
   box — every trend links to its source."
3. **Read a technology (90s).** Technology tool: type their field → CPC classes → the
   improvement-rate trajectory + accelerating/maturing verdict + four-tier lead-time
   sparklines. Point out the greyed "not yet reliable" zone: *"it tells you when it doesn't
   know."*
4. **What's moving (60s).** Cluster board: rising vs declining, share-of-voice momentum,
   corroborating sources; click through to a primary source.
5. **Close (30s).** "Free briefing today; the full engine in early access. What field should
   I put on the radar for you first?"

### Discovery questions
- How do you track emerging technologies today, and what does it cost you?
- When a trend call is challenged internally, how do you defend it?
- Which decisions would change if you saw a shift 2–3 years earlier?
- Do you produce trend/foresight deliverables for others (board, clients)?

### Objection handling
- *"Can I trust the numbers?"* → Every number links to its source; the engine withholds
  what it can't prove and we publish our validation (23/24 recovery, 6/6 momentum).
- *"Isn't this just another keyword tool?"* → Keyword tools watch market news only; we read
  science, patents and funding first, on a peer-reviewed method.
- *"Is the method proprietary?"* → No — it's published (SPNP, 2021). Our edge is the corpus,
  calibration, transparency and price. We'd rather be honest than oversell.
- *"How current is it?"* → Direction and momentum are live; absolute improvement rates are
  reliable to ~2019 by design and we grey out the rest. We don't fake recency.
- *"Data privacy?"* → Analysis is 100% local; account data uses named EU processors; no
  tracking cookies.

### Follow-up email
> Thanks for the time, {name}. Here's your field on the radar and the cited cluster summary
> we looked at. The weekly briefing will keep you on the rising clusters in {space}; when
> you'd like the export/dossier, we can open early access. — {sender}
