# Deliverable 3 — Positioning Brief

## 1. Three positioning options (evaluated)

| # | Angle | One-liner | Clarity | Differentiation | Credibility | Conversion | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Technical** — "local-LLM foresight engine on a 109 M-edge patent graph" | "The open, local trend-intelligence engine." | High | Med (engine specs impress few buyers) | High | Low (buries the benefit) | Supporting layer |
| B | **Outcome / lead-time** — "see where a technology is going before the market does, every number traceable" | "See it in the research before you see it in the market." | High | **High** (the lead-time chain + evidence is genuinely rare mid-market) | High (validated + honest) | **High** | **PRIMARY** |
| C | **Category vision** — "foresight as a public utility / democratized foresight" | "Foresight, out of the enterprise and into your hands." | Med | Med (aspirational, weaker proof) | Med (traction not there yet) | Med | Seasoning only |

**Chosen: B (lead-time, evidence-first)**, with A as the "how it works / trust" backbone
and one restrained note of C in the closing CTA. Rationale: B is the only angle that is
simultaneously (1) the product's actual data model, (2) hard to copy at this price point,
(3) fully backed by V-class evidence, and (4) a benefit a buyer feels in one sentence.

## 2. Messaging architecture

### L1 — Category statement
> Catandary is an evidence-based, local-first **trend & foresight intelligence platform**.

### L2 — Value proposition
> For **innovation, strategy and foresight teams** who need to know **where a technology is
> heading before it hits the market**, **Catandary** reads the whole innovation chain —
> **science → patents → funding → market** — and turns **1M+ signals** into trend
> clusters, momentum and technology-improvement rates, **with every number traceable to
> its primary source**. Unlike black-box keyword tools, nothing is asserted without a
> citation you can click; unlike €50k enterprise suites, it runs on local models at a
> self-service price.

### L3 — Hero
- **Eyebrow:** `EVIDENCE-BASED FORESIGHT · SCIENCE → PATENTS → FUNDING → MARKET`
- **Headline:** **See it in the research before you see it in the market.**
- **Subhead:** Catandary reads the whole innovation chain and turns over a million signals
  into trend clusters, momentum and technology-improvement rates — every number traceable
  to the primary source you can click.
- **Primary CTA:** *Get the weekly briefing* (free newsletter — the live conversion)
- **Secondary CTA:** *Explore the live engine →* (`/trends/foresight`)
- **Proof line:** `1,000,000+ signals · 60,000+ articles · 243 primary sources · 100% local`

### L4 — Message pillars

| Pillar | Problem | What Catandary does | Product proof (V#) | Visible effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The whole chain, one corpus** | Market news tells you a trend *after* it arrived | Signals from science, patents, funding and market on one lead-time axis | V3, V16 | You see how *early* a theme is, not just that it's here |

| **5. Local, open, affordable** | Real foresight is enterprise-priced | Runs on local models + open data; self-service tiers | V4, V22, V19 | Board-grade foresight without the enterprise contract |

## 4. Brand voice

**Souverän, präzise, evidenzbasiert.** We sound like an instrument, not a salesperson.

- **Do:** lead with the concrete; name the mechanism; show the source; state limits plainly
  ("reliable to ~2019", "18 areas today"); use short declaratives + one precise technical
  clause.
- **Don't:** use *revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changing, seamless, next-generation,
  powered by AI, unlock your potential, transform your business* — unless immediately
  defined by what it concretely does.
- **Signature move:** every bold claim is followed within one line by its evidence or its
  limit. Honesty is the brand's aesthetic.
- **Tagline options:** *"Evidence, not vibes."* · *"See it early. Prove it."* ·
  *"Foresight you can cite."*
