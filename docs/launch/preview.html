What we don't claim

Methods we use are based on MIT research and others use it too. Absolute rates are reliable only to about 2019. Used with caution and responsibly.

Method · peer-reviewed, shared, with stated limits

Where the limits are

The free feed is machine-assisted: each short article is generated from a single source, checked against that source by automatic gates. It is a 30-day window, not an archive. The client sheets are the opposite: measured, checked, written, read and signed by a person.

Questions

The honest answers.

What is in the database?

The Catandary proprietary database: 21.9 million dated signals since 1990 across four tiers - scholarly works, patent records, public funding records, and trade press across eight industry verticals. Every entry is dated and attributed to its source; every published article keeps the link to the original it was written from.

Do you predict trends?

Not here. We measure what the database shows: direction, breadth, momentum as share of attention, and - for technologies with a deep patent record - a relative improvement-rate reading up to the year the data can support. What that means for your decision is the analysis; what happens next is joint understanding.

Is the method proprietary?

No, and we would be clear about it. The methods are peer-reviewed research from MIT that others use too. What we bring is the database, the calibration, the transparency of the evidence, and a person who reads and reasons it with you.

How are the feed articles written?

The free feed is generated in-house from the source signal, about a hundred words each, and labelled as such. No cloud AI model is involved. A grounding gate holds back any article that introduces a figure or date the source does not contain. Articels are gated, read them as pointers to the source, and follow the link for the source.

What does it cost?

Three fixed prices, invoiced, excl. VAT: a Trajectory Sheet, deep dive for one field 1,490 €; Field Watch for three fields 390 €/month (setup 900 € once, +90 €/month per additional field, twelve months, three-month pilot at 290 €/month); an Analyst Day 1,200 €. The field probe - One pager of the Sheet for your field - is free. No tiers beyond these, no checkout, no self-service.

What is a "field"?

A technology, material, process or market segment in your words - "solid-state fermentation", "LFP cells", "fibre-based packaging". We turn it into a list of search phrases for the database, show you what that selection catches and misses, and you sign it off before anything is measured. The mapping is printed on every sheet.

Is my data private?

There is no account to create. The feed and its search run in your browser; we use no analytics and no tracking cookies. If you subscribe to the briefing, your email address is handled by our delivery processor under the terms in the privacy policy - no open or click tracking.

Who is it for?

Innovation, strategy and R&D teams in SMEs and corporates; consultants who need measured numbers for a client industry; investors checking a technology or a company. Anyone who has to defend a technology call with sources rather than opinion.
