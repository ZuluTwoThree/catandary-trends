# GLP-1 / Incretin Technology: State of Play and a 12-Month Playbook for a Mid-Sized European Food, Nutrition or Health-Tech Company

*Research conducted 06 September 2026. Sources predating 2026 are flagged; most data below is from 2025–2026.*

## 1. Where the science actually stands

The field has moved from single-target GLP-1 agonists to multi-hormone agonists and non-peptide orals. Eli Lilly's triple agonist **retatrutide** (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon) hit up to 28.3% mean weight loss at 80 weeks in Phase 3 **TRIUMPH-1** (a subgroup reached 30.3% at 104 weeks), and Lilly reported positive topline **TRIUMPH-2/TRIUMPH-3** results in cardiometabolic/cardiovascular-disease patients in July 2026, with a BLA planned for Q1 2027 ([Eli Lilly](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-triple-agonist-retatrutide-delivered-powerful-weight-loss); [AJMC](https://www.ajmc.com/view/retatrutide-achieves-up-to-30-3-average-weight-loss-in-phase-3-triumph-1-trial); [Eli Lilly](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-triple-agonist-retatrutide-successful-two-additional)). Lilly's oral, non-peptide pill **orforglipron** (**Foundayo**) was FDA-approved 1 April 2026, the first GLP-1 pill without food/water restrictions, showing 12.4% weight loss at 72 weeks at the top dose ([Eli Lilly](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fda-approves-lillys-foundayotm-orforglipron-only-glp-1-pill); [Patient Care Online](https://www.patientcareonline.com/view/fda-approves-orforglipron-first-oral-glp-1-receptor-agonist-for-weight-loss-with-no-food-or-water-restrictions)). Novo Nordisk's oral **Wegovy pill** (25 mg semaglutide) got EU marketing authorisation in July 2026 and reached over 5 million cumulative US prescriptions within 30 weeks of launch ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327953/0/en/novo-nordisk-receives-european-commission-approval-of-wegovy-pill-as-first-oral-glp-1-for-weight-management-in-the-eu-single-ready-to-use-pen-for-higher-dose-7-2-mg-also-approved.html); [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/novo-nordisk-wegovy-pill-sales-prescriptions-q1-2026/819418/)).

Novo's dual agonist **CagriSema** (semaglutide + cagrilintide) missed non-inferiority against tirzepatide (23% vs. 25.5% weight loss at 84 weeks) but hit 22.7% at 68 weeks and beat semaglutide on HbA1c/weight in diabetes trials (REIMAGINE); a US obesity decision is expected Q4 2026 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/23/novo-nordisk-stock-cagrisema-trial-fails-weight-loss.html); [PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/novo-nordisk-cagrisema-falls-short-25-weight-loss-target-patients-obesity-overweight); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/novo-nordisks-cagrisema-2-4-mg--2-4-mg-demonstrated-significant-reduction-in-hba1c-and-weight-across-multiple-studies-in-the-reimagine-program-presented-at-ada-2026--302793443.html)). Novo's amylin/GLP-1 co-agonist **amycretin** showed up to 14.5% (subcutaneous) and 10.1% (oral) weight loss with HbA1c cuts to 1.8% in a November 2025 Phase 2 diabetes trial, moving to Phase 3 in 2026 ([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/novo-nordisk-phase-2-trial-with-amycretin-reports-significant-weight-loss-and-hba1c-reduction-in-type-2-diabetes)). Boehringer Ingelheim/Zealand Pharma's **survodutide** posted 16.6% weight loss plus 34% visceral-fat and 63% liver-fat reduction in Phase 3 SYNCHRONIZE-1, capping lean-mass loss at 11.3% of total tissue change ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/07/3307740/0/en/zealand-pharma-announces-boehringer-ingelheim-s-survodutide-phase-iii-trial-in-people-living-with-obesity-showed-targeted-34-visceral-and-63-liver-fat-reduction-while-minimizing-le.html)). Zealand/Roche's amylin analog **petrelintide** hit 10.7% weight loss with "placebo-like tolerability" in Phase 2, advancing to Phase 3 in H2 2026 alongside a fixed-dose combo with Roche's CT-388 ([Roche](https://www.roche.com/media/releases/med-cor-2026-03-05); [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/29/3284133/0/en/zealand-pharma-and-roche-to-advance-petrelintide-an-amylin-analog-to-phase-3-trials-for-chronic-weight-management.html)). Structure Therapeutics' oral **aleniglipron** (GSBR-1290) reported 16.3% placebo-adjusted weight loss at 44 weeks with a clean cardiac/hepatic profile ([Clinical Trial Vanguard](https://www.clinicaltrialvanguard.com/article/intel-brief/alenigliprons-phase-2b-data-redraws-the-oral-glp-1-efficacy-map-and-raises-hard-questions-for-phase-3-design/)); Viking Therapeutics completed Phase 3 enrollment for injectable VK2735 in March 2026, with its oral form showing 12.2% loss at 13 weeks ([Viking IR](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2026-03-26-Viking-Therapeutics-Announces-Completion-of-Enrollment-in-Phase-3-VANQUISH-2-Trial-of-VK2735); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/viking-therapeutics-presents-data-from-its-13-week-phase-2-venture-oral-dosing-trial-of-vk2735-at-european-congress-on-obesity-eco-2026-302768959.html)). Amgen's monthly **MariTide** (~20% weight loss without a plateau in Phase 2) has Phase 3 MARITIME studies enrolling through 2026 ([Amgen](https://investors.amgen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/amgen-announces-robust-weight-loss-maritide-people-living/)).

On **muscle preservation**, the Nature Medicine **BELIEVE** trial (Lilly, via its 2023 acquisition of Versanis Bio for up to $1.925bn) combined semaglutide with activin-blocker bimagrumab, driving 22.1% weight loss with 92% from fat mass, versus up to 40% lean-mass loss typical of GLP-1s alone ([EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1118938); [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/News/2023/07/Eli-Lilly-to-Acquire-Versanis-Bio-in-up-to-1-925-Billion-Deal-With-Potential-Implications)). For food formulators, a 2026 Frontiers in Endocrinology scoping review shows dietary fibers can stimulate **endogenous** GLP-1 secretion via microbial fermentation, while a 2025 study found protein *source* mattered less than total protein/energy content for GLP-1/PYY response ([Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/endocrinology/articles/10.3389/fendo.2026.1880500/full); [Springer/EJN](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00394-025-03839-y)).

## 2. Patents and IP

Europe is the key nuance investors and food/health companies get wrong. The core European compound patent (EP 1 863 839) on semaglutide expired March 2026, and the base patent lapses in ~10 countries in 2026 — Canada, Brazil, China, India, Mexico, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, South Africa among them — covering ~44% of world population and ~48% of global obesity burden ([CZ app](https://www.czapp.com/analyst-insights/ozempics-patent-protection-starts-to-expire-in-2026-what-next/); [Reddie & Grose](https://www.reddie.co.uk/2026/02/09/changes-in-the-ozempic-patent-landscape/)). But a country-specific **Supplementary Protection Certificate (SPC)** keeps Novo's monopoly intact in the Netherlands (and much of the EU) until **March 2031** — confirmed 5 August 2026 when The Hague District Court granted Novo a preliminary injunction against Dutch compounder Ceban Ziekenhuisfarmacie over compounded nasal-spray semaglutide ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323414/20260806/dutch-court-blocks-compounded-semaglutide-confirming-europes-generics-lockout-persists.htm)). Novo litigates aggressively elsewhere: it sued Hims & Hers in the US on 9 February 2026 over US Patent 8,129,343 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/09/novo-nordisk-sues-hims-hers-compounded-obesity-drugs.html)), and in October 2025 won ex parte German injunctions (Düsseldorf, Frankfurt) against exhibitors marketing infringing products at the CPHI Frankfurt trade fair ([Techtimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323414/20260806/dutch-court-blocks-compounded-semaglutide-confirming-europes-generics-lockout-persists.htm)). Patent-landscape analytics show GLP-1 patent families climbed from 93 (2019) to a peak of 145 (2022), a 62% rise tracking semaglutide's commercial success, with grant publications hitting a record 119 in 2025 and China now a major filer alongside the US, South Korea, Denmark and Switzerland ([Lexology/PatSeer](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=b712074a-67df-4f9c-bdc2-1be00d4ee61c)). **Bottom line for Europe: cheap generic semaglutide is not coming to the EU before 2031**, unlike India, Brazil or China.

## 3. Funding and M&A

Capital is pouring into incretin biology. Pfizer won a bidding war against Novo Nordisk for **Metsera**, closing at up to $10.2bn/86.25 USD-per-share including CVRs after the FTC flagged antitrust risk in Novo's proposed structure; the fight escalated from Pfizer's original $4.9–7.3bn offer through Novo counter-bids of $6.5bn then $7.6bn ([STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2025/11/07/pfizer-beats-novo-nordisk-to-acquire-metsera-obesity/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/08/metsera-accepts-pfizers-10-billion-bid-in-ongoing-ma-battle.html)). Roche paid $2.7bn for **Carmot Therapeutics** (Dec. 2023), gaining CT-388 (22.5% weight loss at 48 weeks, Phase 2) and oral CT-996 ([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/drug-development/roche-to-accelerate-development-of-obesity-assets-from-2-7b-carmot-buy); [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/roches-glp1gip-drug-carmot-sees-225-weight-loss-readies-phase-3-trial)). Chinese assets are entering the West via "NewCo" licensing: **Kailera Therapeutics** in-licensed three Hengrui candidates (dual agonist ribupatide/HRS9531, oral HRS-7535) for $110m upfront plus ~19.9% Hengrui equity and up to $6bn milestones, then IPO'd for $625m in 2026 ([VCBeat](https://www.vcbeathealth.com/article/3046); [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/Opinion/26/04/52006951/kailera-therapeutics-strong-ipo-validates-newco-strategy)). Italy's **Menarini** licensed EU rights to Gan & Lee's biweekly bofanglutide for up to €726m, one of several Gan & Lee outbound deals (Latin America, India, South Korea) ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/gan-lee-licenses-obesity-glp-1-bofanglutide-to-menarini-for-europe-in-deal-worth-up-to-726m)). European biotech raised record sums: **Verdiva Bio** launched with a $411m Series A in January 2025 and completed Phase 2b enrollment for oral VRB-101 in February 2026 ([BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/verdiva-obesity-biotech-startup-launch-sciwind-china/736880/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260224056167/en/Verdiva-Bio-Announces-Completion-of-Enrollment-for-EVOLVE-2-Phase-2b-Study-of-VRB-101-a-Potential-Once-Weekly-Oral-GLP-1-Peptide-Analog-in-Individuals-with-Obesity)); **Antag Therapeutics** raised €80m (Versant, Novo Holdings) for a GIP-antagonist approach ([BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/antag-versant-novo-gip-antagonist-obesity/734505/)). On manufacturing, Novo Holdings closed its $16.5bn Catalent acquisition, including $11bn for three fill-finish plants (Italy, Belgium, US) to ease supply constraints from 2026 onward ([DCAT](https://www.dcatvci.org/top-industry-news/novo-holdings-completes-16-5-bn-acquisition-of-catalent/)).

## 4. Market signals

Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly remain the commercial duopoly, but the balance has shifted toward Lilly. Lilly's Q2 2026 Mounjaro revenue hit $9.94bn (+91% YoY) and Zepbound $4.93bn (+46%); H1 2026 combined revenue was $27.7bn, and Lilly raised full-year guidance to $85–87bn ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lilly-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-raises-full-year-guidance-and-highlights-continued-growth-and-pipeline-progress-302843165.html); [SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000059478/000005947826000081/lly-20260630.htm)). Novo's obesity-care sales grew 16% in Q2 2026 on the Wegovy pill, but pricing pressure drove a guidance cut, a 21.7% single-day share drop in late July 2026 (~$70bn market cap erased), and a plan under new CEO Mike Doustdar (since August 2025) to cut 9,000 jobs (11% of staff), saving DKK 8bn (~$1.25bn) by end-2026 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/novo-nordisk-stock-guidance-earnings-wegovy-ozempic-eli-lilly.html); [Xtalks](https://xtalks.com/novo-nordisk-layoffs-to-hit-11-of-workforce-in-1-25b-savings-plan-4386/)); Novo's US share has reportedly fallen to ~40% versus Lilly's 60% ([247wallst](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/04/10/eli-lilly-vs-novo-nordisk-the-obesity-drug-race-has-a-new-leader/)). On access: the FDA declared the injectable semaglutide shortage resolved 21 February 2025, with compounding wind-down deadlines of 22 April/22 May 2025 ([Alston & Bird](https://www.alston.com/en/insights/publications/2025/03/fda-resolves-semaglutide-shortage); [Harris Beach Murtha](https://www.harrisbeachmurtha.com/insights/glp-1-weight-loss-drugs-off-shortage-list-deadlines-to-stop-compounding/)). In the UK, NHS/NICE primary-care rollout of tirzepatide expanded from BMI ≥40 (April 2026) to BMI 35–39.9 (23 June 2026), on a pathway NICE says could take up to 12 years to reach everyone eligible; Wegovy stays routed through specialist Tier-3 services ([NHS England](https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/interim-commissioning-guidance-nice-ta1026-tirzepatide/); [glp1compared.co.uk](https://glp1compared.co.uk/blog/nhs-access-guide.html)). France became the first EU country to reimburse Wegovy/Mounjaro for obesity from 15 June 2026 (65% of cost, ~€100m/year budget impact), and Germany's AMNOG process is reportedly under review for broader coverage beyond its current cardiovascular-risk-only threshold ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/health/2026/05/28/france-to-become-the-first-country-in-europe-to-reimburse-weight-loss-drugs); [vasro.de](https://vasro.de/en/amnog-in-practice-germany-evaluates-glp-1-obesity-drugs/); [Medicalbrief](https://www.medicalbrief.co.za/germany-mulls-reimbursing-for-weigh-loss-drugs/)). On CPG: a Cornell/Numerator analysis found households with a GLP-1 user cut grocery spending ~6% within six months (~9% for higher-income households), with savory-snack purchases down 11% and baskets shifting to protein, fiber and seafood ([Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/glp-1-drug-use-cuts-grocery-spending-by-6-study-finds/736313/)); Morgan Stanley projects 24 million US GLP-1 users by 2035 with soft-drink/snack/baked-goods consumption down ~3% ([GroceryDive](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/glp-1-drugs-grocery-stores-impact/736461/)). Nestlé launched "Vital Pursuit" (frozen pizzas, protein pasta, sub-$5 sandwich melts) plus a Nestlé Health Science GLP-1 nutrition platform; Conagra became the first major CPG to label 26 Healthy Choice meals "GLP-1 friendly" ([nutraingredients](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/08/25/glp-1-nutrition-race-heats-up-as-nestle-supplements-and-pharma-target-companion-market/); [tastewise.io](https://tastewise.io/blog/glp-1-friendly-foods)).

## 6. What the evidence does not support

Several claims circulating in trade press and social media outrun the data. **"GLP-1s are longevity drugs"**: peer-reviewed commentary explicitly states long-term randomized trials with mortality as a primary endpoint are still running, and that these drugs were not designed or studied for longevity in otherwise-healthy people — improved healthspan via reduced obesity/diabetes/CVD risk is plausible but not the same as a proven lifespan effect ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42190939/); [Nature Health](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44360-026-00109-x)). **"GLP-1s are proven addiction cures"**: research into alcohol/opioid/nicotine use disorder is described in the literature as "early stage," not an established indication ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12729087/)). **Thyroid cancer, pancreatitis and suicidality "epidemics"**: a 2026 Medscape "myth of the month" review and multiple meta-analyses (93 RCTs on cancer risk; 27 RCTs on suicide/self-harm) found no significant increased risk versus placebo/comparators, though monitoring continues ([Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/myth-month-glp-1s-have-many-dangerous-side-effects-2026a10008zu); [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2026.1736380/full)). **"Cheap generic Ozempic is imminent in Europe"**: false — the EU compound patent's 2026 expiry is offset by SPCs valid to 2031, actively enforced by Novo Nordisk in court ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323414/20260806/dutch-court-blocks-compounded-semaglutide-confirming-europes-generics-lockout-persists.htm)). **"CagriSema is at least as good as tirzepatide"**: the pivotal head-to-head trial explicitly failed non-inferiority ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/23/novo-nordisk-stock-cagrisema-trial-fails-weight-loss.html)).

## 7. Timing

Near-term catalysts for a European mid-sized company to track: CagriSema's US obesity decision (Q4 2026); Lilly's retatrutide BLA (Q1 2027) and continuing Phase 3 cardiometabolic readouts through 2026; orforglipron's EU launch, not expected before 2027 ([Fierce/veloxpeps summary](https://veloxpeps.com/guides/oral-wegovy-eu-approval-2026/)); survodutide's SYNCHRONIZE-2 diabetes data later in 2026; petrelintide and aleniglipron entering Phase 3 in 2026; semaglutide off-patent supply ramping in India/Brazil/China through 2026–2027 while the EU stays protected to 2031; and continued NHS/France/Germany reimbursement expansion through 2026–2027 that will materially grow the eligible European patient pool even without generics.

## 8. Risks and open questions

Key uncertainties: whether Novo's SPC enforcement (Netherlands ruling, German CPHI injunctions) holds up on appeal EU-wide; whether France's reimbursement move pushes Germany, Italy or NICE/Wegovy to broaden faster than signaled; how durable the muscle-preservation edge of survodutide/bimagrumab proves in longer trials; whether retatrutide's/CagriSema's cardiovascular data (TRIUMPH-3, REDEFINE) hold up under full peer review versus topline releases; and how much of the "GLP-1 companion food" trend (Nestlé, Conagra) is durable demand versus repositioning of existing SKUs. I could not find EU-specific compounding enforcement data beyond the Netherlands case, could not verify a 2026 dollar figure for the total European GLP-1-adjacent food/supplement market, and full peer-reviewed publication of TRIUMPH-2/3 and REDEFINE was not yet available as of the research date.

## 9. What a mid-sized European food, nutrition or health-tech company should do in the next 12 months

1. **Reformulate around evidence, not hype.** Prioritize verified satiety/GLP-1 mechanisms — fiber fermentation and adequate total protein/energy per meal — over source-specific protein marketing claims that the 2025 EJN study found don't differentiate GLP-1/PYY response ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00394-025-03839-y)); avoid EU health-claim regulation exposure by not implying a product "boosts GLP-1 like a drug."
2. **Build a "GLP-1 companion" SKU line now**, following Nestlé's Vital Pursuit and Conagra's labeled Healthy Choice approach — small-portion, high-protein, high-fiber, micronutrient-fortified products addressing the documented shift in GLP-1 users' baskets away from savory snacks and carb-heavy staples ([nutraingredients](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/08/25/glp-1-nutrition-race-heats-up-as-nestle-supplements-and-pharma-target-companion-market/); [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/glp-1-drug-use-cuts-grocery-spending-by-6-study-finds/736313/)).
3. **Do not plan around cheap European semaglutide before 2031**; if evaluating compounding, co-packing, or generic-ingredient supply plays, get freedom-to-operate legal review first — Novo Nordisk is litigating aggressively via SPCs even at trade fairs.
4. **Track the France reimbursement precedent** as a leading indicator for Germany/Benelux; a mid-sized nutrition or health-tech company selling into public/insured channels should model a larger reimbursed European GLP-1 population by 2027 and position companion diagnostics/apps (muscle-mass tracking, nutrition coaching) now, mirroring Nestlé Health Science's platform.
5. **Scout licensing/partnership options with clinical-stage players** (e.g., Zealand Pharma, Antag Therapeutics, Verdiva Bio) for adjacent nutrition-science IP (fiber/GLP-1 secretagogues, muscle-preservation nutrition) rather than competing on drug development itself.
6. **Stress-test supply chains** against continued fill-finish capacity constraints; Novo's Catalent-derived capacity only ramps "from 2026 onward," so packaging/CDMO partners serving pharma-adjacent nutrition products may face competing capacity demand.
7. **Avoid marketing or R&D bets on unproven claims** (longevity, addiction-cure positioning) that regulators and fact-checkers are actively pushing back on — reputational and regulatory risk outweighs any short-term marketing upside.
