## What the evidence does not support

Some sources suggest that food can "mimic" or "boost" GLP-1 effects, [Beyond GLP-1 Agonists: Plant-Derived Bioactive Compounds as Adjunctive Strategies for Obesity Management - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13415615/), while other sources state that food alone is unlikely to replicate the satiety effects of GLP-1 drugs [Satiety-Enhancing Foods: A Practical Alternative to GLP-1 Drugs](https://catandary.de/trends/satiety-enhancing-foods-a-practical-alternative-to-glp-1-drugs-33635). The evidence supports the latter: food can support the user, but it is not a substitute for the pharmacological effect.
