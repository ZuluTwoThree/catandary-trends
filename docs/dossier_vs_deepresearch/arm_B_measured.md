# GLP-1 / Incretin Technology: State of Play and the 12-Month Window for a Mid-Sized European Food, Nutrition, or Health-Tech Company

## Executive Summary

The GLP-1/incretin technology field is in a **late-acceleration phase** where the underlying drug science is maturing rapidly (oral small molecules, triple agonists) while the adjacent food and nutrition market is just entering early commercialization. For a mid-sized European company, the next 12 months are defined by a specific regulatory constraint, a clear product design mandate, and a timing gap that favors agility over scale.

The core finding is that the opportunity is **not** to compete with pharmaceutical giants on the drug itself, but to own the **"companion" nutrition layer** that the drug creates a need for. The evidence supports a strategic pivot toward smaller, high-protein, high-fiber portions that address the physiological realities of GLP-1 users (reduced appetite, muscle loss risk, GI side effects) without relying on prohibited "GLP-1 friendly" health claims in the EU.

3.  **Do** prepare for a step-change in European adoption in 2026–2027, driven by the arrival of oral drugs and expanded insurance coverage (e.g., Germany’s GKV), which will expand the user base significantly [Transformative or overhyped? The impact of weight-loss drugs on European food demand | articles | ING THINK](https://think.ing.com/articles/transformative-or-overhyped-the-impact-of-weight-loss-drugs-on-european-food-demand1/), .
4.  **Do** explore "precision nutrition" as a differentiator, using biomarkers and AI to tailor dietary intake to GLP-1 therapy, though this remains an emerging area with limited prospective clinical evidence [Precision Nutrition Integration Targets GLP-1 Therapy Optimization](https://catandary.de/trends/precision-nutrition-integration-targets-glp-1-therapy-optimization-23833573), [Integrating Precision Nutrition with GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Therapy: Mechanisms, Clinical Outcomes, and Pharmacoeconomic Implications - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13516115/).

## 1. What Is Actually Moving: Science, Patents, Funding, and Market Signals

### 1.1 Scientific Frontier: From Injectable Peptides to Oral Small Molecules and Multi-Agonists

The drug science is moving decisively away from injectable peptides toward oral small molecules and multi-agonist therapies. This shift is critical for the food sector because it changes the user profile, the frequency of administration, and the side-effect profile.

*   **Oral Small Molecules:** Orforglipron, an oral small-molecule GLP-1 receptor agonist, has demonstrated Phase 2 and Phase 3 efficacy comparable to injectable drugs, removing the need for cold-chain storage and needles [Orforglipron, an Oral Small-Molecule GLP-1 Receptor Agonist for Obesity Treatment](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2511774), [Daily Oral GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Orforglipron for Adults with Obesity](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2302392), [Eli Lilly's Pill Orforglipron Challenges the Injection Pen in Phase II Weight-Loss Trial](https://catandary.de/trends/the-oral-revolution-rethinking-glp-1-delivery-models-50824). This is a major barrier to entry for broader consumer adoption, particularly in Europe where oral drugs are expected to become the predominant form by 2027 [Transformative or overhyped? The impact of weight-loss drugs on European food demand | articles | ING THINK](https://think.ing.com/articles/transformative-or-overhyped-the-impact-of-weight-loss-drugs-on-european-food-demand1/).
*   **Triple Agonists:** The field is shifting from simple weight reduction to "comprehensive metabolic management." Eli Lilly’s retatrutide (a triple agonist) has shown Phase 3 data indicating benefits for metabolic syndrome comorbidities like sleep apnea and osteoarthritis, not just weight loss [Beyond Weight Loss: The Clinical Expansion of Multi-Agonist Obesity Therapeutics](https://catandary.de/trends/beyond-weight-loss-the-clinical-expansion-of-multi-agonist-obesity-the-48269). Similarly, Novo Nordisk’s UBT251 achieved a mean weight loss of 19.7% in a Phase 2 trial in China [Novo Nordisk’s triple agonist delivers up to 19.7% average weight loss in Phase 2 trial](https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/novo-nordisks-triple-agonist-delivers-up-to-19-7-average-weight-loss-in-phase-2-trial/).
*   **Mechanistic Reappraisal:** Future directions in incretin research are shaped by three major trends: multi-receptor agonism, oral drug development, and the mechanistic reappraisal of GIP physiology within an integrated enteroinsular network [Recent advances in incretin biology and therapeutics: From glucose‐dependent insulinotropic polypeptide reappraisal to next‐generation agonists](https://doi.org/10.1111/jdi.70299).

### 2.1 Market Size and Adoption Lag

The European market is currently small but poised for a step-change.

*   **Insurance Expansion:** Germany’s statutory health insurers (GKV) expanded obesity drug coverage in early 2025, adding an estimated 1.2 million eligible patients annually to the European user base . This is a critical driver of the 2026–2027 acceleration.

### 2.2 Regulatory Barriers: The "GLP-1 Friendly" Claim Problem

A significant regulatory barrier exists in the EU.

*   **EU Regulation:** In the EU, any claim that a product is "GLP-1 friendly" is considered either a disease-related or a health-related claim [How 'GLP-1 friendly' claims are regulated in the UK and EU](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/07/glp-1-friendly-claims-how-theyre-regulated/). Disease-related claims cannot be used for either food or food supplements. Health-related claims can only be used if approved by the EU. Currently, no application to use the phrase "GLP-1 friendly" has been filed, and legal experts consider approval unlikely [How 'GLP-1 friendly' claims are regulated in the UK and EU](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/07/glp-1-friendly-claims-how-theyre-regulated/).
*   **UK Regulation:** In the UK, food labelling law restricts references to preventing, treating, or curing a human disease. Whether "GLP-1 friendly" claims contravene this is untested in courts, but it is potentially arguable [How 'GLP-1 friendly' claims are regulated in the UK and EU](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/07/glp-1-friendly-claims-how-theyre-regulated/).
*   **Implication:** European companies cannot use the "GLP-1 friendly" label on packaging. They must use indirect marketing and focus on nutrient density rather than drug-specific claims [How 'GLP-1 friendly' claims are regulated in the UK and EU](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/07/glp-1-friendly-claims-how-theyre-regulated/).

### 2.3 Product Design Mandate: Smaller, High-Protein, High-Fiber

The physiological needs of GLP-1 users dictate a clear product design mandate.

*   **Protein Intake:** The 2025–2030 US Dietary Guidelines recommend a significant increase in protein intake (1.2–1.6 g/kg/day) and a reduction in processed foods [New US dietary guidelines elevate meat and full-fat dairy, ignite protein debate: ‘Most Americans already eat plenty’](https://agfundernews.com/new-us-dietary-guidelines-elevate-meat-and-full-fat-dairy-ignite-protein-debate-most-americans-already-eat-plenty), [Dietary guidelines declare war on processed foods and sugar, encourage more protein and dairy](https://www.statnews.com/2026/01/07/dietary-guidelines-revised-more-protein-less-sugar-highly-processed-foods/). This trend is accelerating protein reformulation in the food industry [Dietary Guideline Release Drives Protein and Fiber Concentration](https://catandary.de/trends/dietary-guideline-release-drives-protein-and-fiber-concentration-24743601).
*   **Mechanistic Basis:** Research indicates that specific dietary amino acids (leucine, lysine, phenylalanine) can stimulate the secretion of satiety hormones (CCK) and incretins (GLP-1), providing a mechanistic basis for protein-focused nutrition strategies for GLP-1 users [Leucine (and lysine) increased plasma levels of the satiety hormone cholecystokinin (CCK), and phenylalanine of the incretin glucagon-like peptide 1 (GLP-1) after oral gavages in pigs](https://doi.org/10.1093/jas/skad175).
*   **Portion Size:** Users want smaller and more nutrient-dense portions [How 'GLP-1 friendly' claims are regulated in the UK and EU](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/07/glp-1-friendly-claims-how-theyre-regulated/). Ready meals and high-protein snacks are gaining traction in between-meal and low-appetite use occasions [GLP-1 Diet Food Market Size, Share & Forecast to 2036 | FMI](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/glp-1-diet-food-market).
*   **GI Tolerance:** Fiber-forward and gut-support products are gaining relevance where users need gentler digestion support or more complete nutrition [GLP-1 Diet Food Market Size, Share & Forecast to 2036 | FMI](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/glp-1-diet-food-market).
