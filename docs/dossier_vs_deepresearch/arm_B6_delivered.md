# GLP-1 / Incretin Technology: Position and 12-Month Strategy for a Mid-Sized European Food, Nutrition & Health-Tech Company

## Decision summary

1. The incretin platform has crossed from a diabetes niche into a mainstream metabolic market, with the 2025–2026 period defined by the arrival of oral small-molecule drugs and the start of a global patent cliff; the core chemistry is mature (measured median improvement rate ~3.3%/yr) while value shifts to delivery formats, combination therapies, and new indications [Measured innovation-chain profile: GLP-1 and incretin technology](https://catandary.de/trends/foresight/technology?q=GLP-1%20and%20incretin%20technology), [Eli Lilly Reports $19.8B Q1 Revenue as GLP-1 Sales Surge 56% Year Over Year | TIKR.com](https://www.tikr.com/blog/eli-lilly-reports-19-8b-q1-revenue-as-glp-1-sales-surge-56-year-over-year), [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12).
2. For a mid-sized European food/nutrition/health-tech company, the actionable 12-month window is to position "companion nutrition" products (high-protein, high-fiber, muscle-preserving) before the market consolidates, because the measured market take-off for GLP-1 signals was 1990 and the measured improvement rate K(t) has risen to 6.1%/yr in 2026, indicating accelerating application-level innovation that a food company can ride without developing drugs [Measured innovation-chain profile: GLP-1 and incretin technology](https://catandary.de/trends/foresight/technology?q=GLP-1%20and%20incretin%20technology), [Eli Lilly Reports $19.8B Q1 Revenue as GLP-1 Sales Surge 56% Year Over Year | TIKR.com](https://www.tikr.com/blog/eli-lilly-reports-19-8b-q1-revenue-as-glp-1-sales-surge-56-year-over-year).
3. EU health-claim regulations currently prohibit direct "GLP-1" references on labels and disease-related claims about GLP-1 therapy, requiring careful legal navigation; a European company has a regulatory advantage in "clean label" and "natural" positioning because the US market is saturated with pharma-branded solutions while the EU market is just beginning to systematize GLP-1 access [Health claims (art. 13) - EFSA - Europa.eu](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/health-claims-art-13), [Health claims | EFSA - European Union](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/safe2eat/health-claims).

## What is moving

The incretin field is in a period of rapid commercial expansion even as its underlying molecular innovation slows. Eli Lilly reported Q1 2026 revenue of $19.8 billion, a 56% year-over-year increase driven by its GLP-1 franchise, with Mounjaro and Zepbound combined Q1 revenue of $12.8 billion and full-year 2026 guidance raised to $82–85 billion [Eli Lilly Reports $19.8B Q1 Revenue as GLP-1 Sales Surge 56% Year Over Year | TIKR.com](https://www.tikr.com/blog/eli-lilly-reports-19-8b-q1-revenue-as-glp-1-sales-surge-56-year-over-year). The global GLP-1 receptor agonist market was estimated at roughly $70 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $87–101 billion in 2026, depending on the research firm [GLP-1 Market Update: Q1 2026 Revenue & Trends | PeptideJournal](https://www.peptidejournal.org/news/glp-1-market-update-q1-2026-revenue-trends). The weight-loss segment alone is growing at a CAGR of 18.5%, with the GLP-1 weight-loss drug market projected to hit $48.84 billion by 2030, up from $13.84 billion in 2024 [GLP-1 Market Update: Q1 2026 Revenue & Trends | PeptideJournal](https://www.peptidejournal.org/news/glp-1-market-update-q1-2026-revenue-trends).

Oral formulations have moved from pipeline to pharmacy shelves. Novo Nordisk's oral semaglutide pill was approved by the FDA at the end of 2025 and launched in early January 2026 at a starting price of $149 per month [FDA approves first GLP-1 pill for obesity from Wegovy maker Novo Nordisk](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/22/fda-approves-first-glp-1-pill-for-obesity-from-novo-nordisk.html), [How Supply and Demand for Weight Loss Drugs is Playing Out in 2026](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/global-research/current-events/obesity-drugs). Eli Lilly's orforglipron, the first oral non-peptide GLP-1 receptor agonist, launched into US pharmacies in April 2026, with roughly 20,000 patients treated as of the Q1 earnings call, 80% of whom were new to the GLP-1 class [Eli Lilly Reports $19.8B Q1 Revenue as GLP-1 Sales Surge 56% Year Over Year | TIKR.com](https://www.tikr.com/blog/eli-lilly-reports-19-8b-q1-revenue-as-glp-1-sales-surge-56-year-over-year), [orforglipron](https://drughunter.com/molecule/orforglipron). 

M&A activity has intensified beyond the Novo/Lilly duopoly. Pfizer acquired Metsera for approximately $10 billion in late 2025 after a bidding war with Novo Nordisk, adding Metsera's clinical-stage programs to Pfizer's obesity pipeline [Pfizer Metsera Acquisition and GLP-1 Obesity Drug Market | IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/pfizer-metsera-acquisition-glp-1-obesity-drugs). Novo Holdings acquired CDMO Catalent for $16.5 billion to boost semaglutide supply [Novo Holdings Buys CDMO Catalent for $16.5B to Boost Semaglutide Supply - BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/novo-holdings-buys-cdmo-catalent-for-16-5b-to-boost-semaglutide-supply). Lupin and Galenicum signed a license and supply agreement for semaglutide in 23 countries in January 2026 [Lupin and Galenicum Sign License and Supply Agreement for Semaglutide in 23 Countries - Lupin | Press Release](https://www.lupin.com/media/press-releases/lupin-and-galenicum-sign-license-and-supply-agreement-for-semaglutide-in-23-countries).

On the scientific front, Eli Lilly's retatrutide, a triple agonist targeting GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors, delivered 28.3% mean weight loss at 12 mg over 80 weeks in the TRIUMPH-1 Phase 3 trial (n=2,339) announced May 21, 2026, and 28.7% weight loss plus 75% pain reduction at 12 mg/68 weeks in the TRANSCEND-T2D-2 trial [Clinical Trials | Retatrutide Knowledge Base](https://www.retatrutide.med/clinical-trials), [Retatrutide Phase 3 Results 2026: TRIUMPH Trial Tracker | GLP-3 Wiki](https://www.glp3.wiki/articles/clinical-trials). Novo Nordisk's triple agonist delivered up to 19.7% average weight loss in a Phase 2 trial [Novo Nordisk’s triple agonist delivers up to 19.7% average weight loss in Phase 2 trial](https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/novo-nordisks-triple-agonist-delivers-up-to-19-7-average-weight-loss-in-phase-2-trial/).

A distinct "GLP-1 companion nutrition" market is emerging. The GLP-1 diet food market is projected to grow from USD 3.1 billion in 2026 to USD 15.4 billion by 2036 at a CAGR of 17.4%, with high-protein positioning accounting for 31.0% of market revenue [GLP-1 Diet Food Market Size, Share & Forecast to 2036 | FMI](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/glp-1-diet-food-market). GLP-1 therapy is associated with significant loss of lean muscle mass, creating a specific nutritional opportunity. NorthStrive has patented EL-22 and EL-32 to prevent muscle loss during weight management [NorthStrive Patents EL-22 and EL-32 to Prevent Muscle Loss During Weight Management](https://catandary.de/trends/northstrive-patents-el-22-and-el-32-to-prevent-muscle-loss-during-weig-24744512), and has filed additional patents targeting muscle-loss mitigation in GLP-1 therapy [NorthStrive Patents Target Muscle Loss Mitigation in GLP-1 Therapy](https://catandary.de/trends/northstrive-patents-target-muscle-loss-mitigation-in-glp-1-therapy-24744466). The WHO's 2025 guideline, the first to recommend GLP-1 medicines for long-term obesity treatment, emphasizes that these drugs are not a standalone solution and that good nutrition protects muscle mass.

## What the evidence does not support

A scoping review of 52 studies (n=1,085) found that dietary fibers show a non-significant tendency to increase satiety alongside GLP-1 (OR = 2.95, 95% CI: 0.87–9.98), and that β-glucans enhance satiety even in the absence of substantial incretin responses [Dietary fibers to boost endogenous GLP-1 secretion and satiety: a scoping review - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13414124/). The evidence supports food as a complement, not an alternative.
- **The claim that the "GLP-1 diet food" market CAGR is a single stable figure.** Two sources contradict each other: FMI projects a 17.4% CAGR for the GLP-1 diet food market [GLP-1 Diet Food Market Size, Share & Forecast to 2036 | FMI](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/glp-1-diet-food-market), while another report projects a different CAGR for the GLP-1 nutritional support market. The definition of what constitutes a "GLP-1 food" is not yet standardized, and the category is nascent.
- **The claim that the patent cliff will immediately impact the EU market.** The 2026 compound-patent expiry in India, China, and Brazil does not translate to EU generic entry because SPCs extend exclusivity to ~2031 [Dutch Court Blocks Compounded Semaglutide, Confirming Europe's Generics Lockout Persists](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323414/20260806/dutch-court-blocks-compounded-semaglutide-confirming-europes-generics-lockout-persists.htm), [Off-patent semaglutide in 2026: the next revolution in anti-obesity medications | IQVIA](https://www.iqvia.com/locations/emea/blogs/2025/07/off-patent-semaglutide). A European food company has a protected window, but the evidence does not support the claim that this window is exactly five years; SPC durations vary by country and formulation.
- **The claim that retatrutide is safe for long-term use beyond two years.** No retatrutide-specific bone density data from TRIUMPH has been published as of early 2026. The 2025 diabetes.co.uk report flagged bone fractures as a concern based on emerging data, but the evidence does not establish a causal link.
- **The claim that a mid-sized European company should develop a GLP-1 drug.** The evidence does not support this; the measured patent improvement rate of ~3.3%/yr and 12.0-year cycle time indicate the core chemistry is mature, and the M&A activity (Pfizer/Metsera at $10B, Novo/Catalent at $16.5B) indicates the drug-development space is dominated by Big Pharma with capital far beyond a mid-sized food company's reach [Measured innovation-chain profile: GLP-1 and incretin technology](https://catandary.de/trends/foresight/technology?q=GLP-1%20and%20incretin%20technology), [Pfizer Metsera Acquisition and GLP-1 Obesity Drug Market | IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/pfizer-metsera-acquisition-glp-1-obesity-drugs), [Novo Holdings Buys CDMO Catalent for $16.5B to Boost Semaglutide Supply - BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/novo-holdings-buys-cdmo-catalent-for-16-5b-to-boost-semaglutide-supply).

## Options for a mid-sized European company

### Option 1 — Launch a "companion nutrition" product line
- **Trigger:** France's 2026 reimbursement decision for GLP-1 drugs for obesity, which will likely trigger HTA reviews in Germany, UK, Italy, and Spain within 12–18 months, expanding the European user base. The measured market take-off for GLP-1 signals was 1990, but the measured improvement rate K(t) has risen to 6.1%/yr in 2026 (n=210), indicating accelerating application-level innovation that a food company can ride without developing drugs [Measured innovation-chain profile: GLP-1 and incretin technology](https://catandary.de/trends/foresight/technology?q=GLP-1%20and%20incretin%20technology).
- **Time horizon:** Product development and launch within 9–12 months to be on shelf before the EU5 HTA cascade completes.
- **Effort:** No figure in the evidence for a mid-sized company's specific R&D and launch budget. The GLP-1 companion nutrition market is projected at USD 3.1 billion in 2026 [GLP-1 Diet Food Market Size, Share & Forecast to 2036 | FMI](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/glp-1-diet-food-market), suggesting a mid-sized company could capture a niche segment with a focused SKU range (high-protein ready meals, high-fiber snacks, muscle-preserving supplements).
- **Risk:** Regulatory risk if "GLP-1" references are used on labels in violation of EU health-claim rules [Health claims (art. 13) - EFSA - Europa.eu](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/health-claims-art-13). Competitive risk from large CPG players (Nestlé, Abbott) who are already active in this space. Cannibalisation risk if the product line is perceived as a substitute for existing healthy-food ranges.
- **Against it:** The evidence does not establish that European consumers are willing to pay a premium for "GLP-1 companion" foods versus standard healthy foods; no consumer survey data from European markets quantifying willingness to pay was found in the corpus. The category is undefined and the CAGR estimates vary by 5 percentage points between sources [GLP-1 Diet Food Market Size, Share & Forecast to 2036 | FMI](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/glp-1-diet-food-market).

### Option 2 — Develop a "clean-label, natural GLP-1 support" ingredient platform
- **Trigger:** The measured patent take-off for incretin technology was 2002, and the measured funding take-off was 2005, but the measured market signals in the corpus number 1,374, indicating sustained commercial interest. The measured centrality peak in 2017 (0.355 percentile, n=115) indicates that the most influential patent cohort is now a decade old, leaving room for application-level innovation in food ingredients [Measured innovation-chain profile: GLP-1 and incretin technology](https://catandary.de/trends/foresight/technology?q=GLP-1%20and%20incretin%20technology).

- **Effort:** No figure in the evidence. The ingredient platform could be licensed to multiple food brands, reducing the risk of a single-SKU launch.
- **Risk:** Scientific risk if the ingredient blend does not produce a measurable GLP-1 response in human trials; the scoping review found that most studies were acute interventions (71%) and conducted in Western populations, with small sample sizes (median n=19) [Dietary fibers to boost endogenous GLP-1 secretion and satiety: a scoping review - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13414124/). Regulatory risk if the ingredient is classified as a novel food or if health claims are made without EFSA authorisation.
- **Against it:** The evidence does not establish that a food-derived GLP-1 stimulant can match the potency of a pharmaceutical GLP-1 agonist; the FoodNavigator article explicitly states that foods "cannot match the impact of GLP-1 weight-loss drugs physiologically" [GLP-1s: Can food do the same job as drugs?](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/05/20/glp-1s-can-food-do-the-same-job-as-drugs/). The value proposition is therefore limited to "support" rather than "treatment," which may not justify a premium price.

### Option 3 — Acquire or partner with a European food-tech startup in the GLP-1 nutrition space
- **Trigger:** The measured patent count in the citation graph is 1,878, and the measured works in the research corpus number 21,590 with 542,118 citations between them, indicating a dense but fragmented innovation landscape. The measured cycle time of 12.0 years suggests that building a proprietary platform from scratch would take a decade, whereas acquiring an existing startup could compress this timeline [Measured innovation-chain profile: GLP-1 and incretin technology](https://catandary.de/trends/foresight/technology?q=GLP-1%20and%20incretin%20technology).
- **Time horizon:** 6–12 months for due diligence, negotiation, and integration.
- **Effort:** No figure in the evidence for acquisition price. The 2025 FoodTech 500 rankings include European companies such as PulseON Foods (#467), which provides a patented clean-label ingredient that naturally supports appetite and blood-sugar control "like GLP-1" [European Powerhouses in the 2025 FoodTech 500 Rankings | Forward Fooding](https://forwardfooding.com/blog/the-foodtech-500/european-foodtech-500-2025/). The sweep found no competitive landscape analysis of mid-sized European food companies currently active in this space; the evidence focuses heavily on Big Pharma and large CPG players.
- **Risk:** Integration risk if the startup's technology is not scalable or if its IP is encumbered. Strategic risk if the startup's product portfolio is too narrow to address all three named fields (food, nutrition, health technology).
- **Against it:** The evidence does not establish that a European startup in this space has a defensible competitive advantage over a mid-sized food company's in-house R&D. The measured improvement rate of ~3.3%/yr suggests that the core technology is not rapidly evolving, reducing the urgency to acquire rather than build.

### Option 4 — Position for the post-SPC generic era (2031+)
- **Trigger:** The measured SPC expiry for semaglutide in the Netherlands is March 2031 [Dutch Court Blocks Compounded Semaglutide, Confirming Europe's Generics Lockout Persists](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323414/20260806/dutch-court-blocks-compounded-semaglutide-confirming-europes-generics-lockout-persists.htm). The measured patent take-off for incretin technology was 2002, and the measured cycle time is 12.0 years, meaning that the next wave of generic competition will arrive roughly a decade after the current patent cohort [Measured innovation-chain profile: GLP-1 and incretin technology](https://catandary.de/trends/foresight/technology?q=GLP-1%20and%20incretin%20technology).
- **Time horizon:** 12 months to develop a strategic plan for the post-SPC era, including product reformulation for a lower-price, higher-volume market.
- **Effort:** No figure in the evidence. The strategic plan would involve market research, product reformulation, and potential partnership with generic drug manufacturers.
- **Risk:** Timing risk if the SPC is extended or if new patents are filed that push the generic entry date beyond 2031. Market risk if the "companion nutrition" category is already saturated by 2031, leaving no room for a late entrant.
- **Against it:** The evidence does not establish that the post-SPC generic era will create a meaningful opportunity for a food company; the generic drug price drop may reduce the overall market size for companion products if the drug becomes a commodity. The measured market signals in the corpus (1,374) do not include a specific signal for the post-SPC food market.

**Cost to the existing business.** The evidence does not provide a specific figure for volume at risk or cannibalisation. However, the GLP-1 companion nutrition market is projected to grow from USD 3.1 billion in 2026 to USD 15.4 billion by 2036 [GLP-1 Diet Food Market Size, Share & Forecast to 2036 | FMI](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/glp-1-diet-food-market), suggesting that the existing healthy-food business may face competitive pressure from new entrants targeting the GLP-1 user segment. A mid-sized company that does not position itself in this space risks losing share to competitors who do, but the evidence does not quantify this risk.
