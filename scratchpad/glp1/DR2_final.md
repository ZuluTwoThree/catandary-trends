## Decision summary

Oral GLP-1 receptor agonists are now on market: orforglipron was published as approved for obesity in *NEJM* in September 2025 [Orforglipron, an Oral Small-Molecule GLP-1 Receptor Agonist for Obesity Treatment](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2511774), and the first oral GLP-1 for type 2 diabetes (semaglutide) was EMA-authorised in January 2020 [First oral GLP-1 treatment for type 2 diabetes — European Medicines Agency (EMA)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/first-oral-glp-1-treatment-type-2-diabetes); semaglutide's compound patent expires in 2026 in several countries, including India and China [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12).

In the European Union, Supplementary Protection Certificates extend drug exclusivity beyond the 20-year patent term to compensate for regulatory review time [Supplementary protection certificates and their impact ... - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6958714/)[Supplementary Protection Certificates - GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/supplementary-protection-certificates), while the non-EU markets identified above lose semaglutide exclusivity in 2026 [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12).

No "GLP-1," "glucagon-like peptide," or "incretin" claim appears on the EU authorised health-claims register [EU register of health claims - Food Safety - European Commission](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims/eu-register-health-claims_en); Regulation (EC) 1924/2006, adopted 20 December 2006, requires EFSA evaluation and Commission authorisation before any health claim may appear on food in the EU [Health claims according to Article 13 of the EC Regulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2606982/).

## What is moving

**Science.** In September 2025, Eli Lilly's oral small-molecule GLP-1 agonist orforglipron was published as approved for obesity treatment in *NEJM* [Orforglipron, an Oral Small-Molecule GLP-1 Receptor Agonist for Obesity Treatment](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2511774); the first oral GLP-1 for T2D, semaglutide (Rybelsus), was EMA-authorised on 31 January 2020 [First oral GLP-1 treatment for type 2 diabetes — European Medicines Agency (EMA)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/first-oral-glp-1-treatment-type-2-diabetes). A narrative review of single, dual, and triple receptor agonists for T2D and obesity was published in 2024 [GLP-1 single, dual, and triple receptor agonists for treating type 2 diabetes and obesity: a narrative review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eclinm.2024.102782).

**Patents.** Novo Nordisk's semaglutide compound patent expires in 2026 in several countries, including India and China [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12). New filings continue: a GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple-agonist composition patent was filed 16 January 2026 [Incretin analogs as GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptor triple agonists and uses thereof](https://patents.google.com/patent/TW202602912A), and a swallowable capsule for stimulating incretin production was filed 16 July 2026 [Swallowable capsule and method for stimulating incretin production within the intestinal tract](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260199673A1). The measured patent improvement rate for the GLP-1/incretin class is a median of 3.3 %/yr across 1,878 patents (2005–2026), indicating a slowly maturing core technology.

**Funding.** Kailera Therapeutics launched with $400 million in October 2024 after licensing four GLP-1 candidates from Jiangsu Hengrui in May 2024 [GLP-1 start-up launches with $400 million - C&EN](https://cen.acs.org/business/start-ups/GLP-1-startlaunches-400-million/102/i31). The EU's Horizon Europe programme (2021–2027, €95.5 billion) is in its final funding window [Funding programmes and open calls - Research and innovation](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/funding/funding-opportunities/funding-programmes-and-open-calls_en), and the Commission proposed a successor 2028–2034 framework with a €175 billion budget on 16 July 2025 [Horizon Europe - Research and innovation - European Commission](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/funding/funding-opportunities/funding-programmes-and-open-calls/horizon-europe_en).

**Market.** In 2024, Novo Nordisk generated $26 billion from Wegovy and Ozempic, and Eli Lilly's Mounjaro and Zepbound had combined sales of nearly $16 billion [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12). In the US, the FDA clarified compounding policies on 1 April 2026 as the national GLP-1 shortage was declared resolved [FDA clarifies policies for compounders as national GLP-1 supply begins to stabilize — FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-clarifies-policies-compounders-national-glp-1-supply-begins-stabilize). The "GLP-1-friendly" food category is emerging: Conagra rolled out "GLP-1 friendly" labels in December 2024 (secondary source only) [Conagra Rolls Out ‘GLP-1 Friendly’ Labels on Food Packaging to Attract Ozempic Users](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/conagra-healthy-choice-glp-1-friendly-label-ozempic-on-track/), and Arla Foods has filed patents on whey-based ingredients for GLP-1-related benefits (secondary source only) [GLP-1 Food Landscape: 4 Trends Making Impact in 2026 - GreyB](https://food.greyb.com/blog/glp-1-food-landscape/).

## Regulatory and IP status

**Patent expiry and SPC in Europe.** Semaglutide's compound patent expires in 2026 in several countries, including India and China [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12). In the EU, Supplementary Protection Certificates under Regulation 469/2009/EC extend exclusivity beyond the 20-year patent term to compensate for the time taken to obtain a marketing authorisation [Supplementary protection certificates and their impact ... - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6958714/). The European Patent Office, created in 1977 under the EPC, grants unitary patents valid only in contracting states; EU patent law remains national and is not fully harmonised [Pharmaceutical patenting in the European Union: reform or ...](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8592279/). UK SPCs operate under separate domestic legislation [Supplementary Protection Certificates - GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/supplementary-protection-certificates). For orforglipron, a dosage-regime patent (WO2024129676A1) is on file [WO2024129676A1 - Dosage regime of orforglipron for treating a subject with type 2 diabetes (t2d), obesity, or overweight with at least one weight related comorbidity - Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024129676A1/en), but no SPC grant or expiry date for orforglipron in the EU was found in the sweep. The specific SPC expiry date for semaglutide in the EU is not stated in the primary evidence.

**Marketing authorisations and pending decisions.** The EMA authorised the first oral GLP-1 (semaglutide/Rybelsus) for T2D on 31 January 2020 [First oral GLP-1 treatment for type 2 diabetes — European Medicines Agency (EMA)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/first-oral-glp-1-treatment-type-2-diabetes). In May 2026, the EMA listed Liraglutide STADA as a hybrid medicine marketing authorisation [Liraglutide STADA — European Medicines Agency (EMA)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/liraglutide-stada), indicating at least one liraglutide hybrid pathway is active in the EU. In June 2024, the EMA issued a communication on EU actions to tackle shortages of GLP-1 receptor agonists [EU actions to tackle shortages of GLP-1 receptor agonists — European Medicines Agency (EMA)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/eu-actions-tackle-shortages-glp-1-receptor-agonists). No pending EMA decision with a stated future date for a new GLP-1 product was identified.

**Court decisions and injunctions.** MDL-3094, consolidated in the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, covers products-liability litigation involving GLP-1 RA products [MDL — Eastern District of Pennsylvania — United States District Court](https://www.paed.uscourts.gov/mdl/mdl-3094-re-glucagon-peptide-1-receptor-agonists-glp-1-ras-products-liability-litigation). The sweep found no primary-source record of a patent-infringement injunction specific to GLP-1s in the EU; Novo Nordisk's reported legal wins against US compounders in April 2025 rest on a press release (secondary source only) [Novo Nordisk protects US patients with legal wins against compounders, including ruling that permanently prohibits compounding pharmacy from selling illegitimate, knockoff Wegovy® or Ozempic®](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/novo-nordisk-protects-us-patients-with-legal-wins-against-compounders-including-ruling-that-permanently-prohibits-compounding-pharmacy-from-selling-illegitimate-knockoff-wegovy-or-ozempic-302438372.html).

**Permitted product claims (EU vs. rest of world).** Under Regulation (EC) 1924/2006, adopted 20 December 2006 [Health claims according to Article 13 of the EC Regulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2606982/), any health or disease-related claim on food requires EFSA scientific evaluation and a Commission implementing act adding it to the Article 13 register [EU register of health claims - Food Safety - European Commission](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims/eu-register-health-claims_en). Outside the EU, no equivalent pre-market claim authorisation exists: Conagra's December 2024 "GLP-1 friendly" labelling (secondary source only) [Conagra Rolls Out ‘GLP-1 Friendly’ Labels on Food Packaging to Attract Ozempic Users](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/conagra-healthy-choice-glp-1-friendly-label-ozempic-on-track/) required no prior regulatory clearance.

## Options for a mid-sized European company

### Option 1 — Launch a high-protein, reduced-volume food line for GLP-1 users (Food)

- **Trigger:** A 2026 peer-reviewed framework identifies specific dietary needs of GLP-1 users—reduced appetite, GI tolerability, protein at 1.5–1.6 g/kg/day—and a 2026 companion paper maps micronutrient risk with incretin agonists in obesity [Nutrition-First Support for GLP-1 and Dual Incretin Therapy in Obesity: A Practical Framework for Dietary Management, Symptom Tolerability, and Long-Term Weight Maintenance](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18111751)[Micronutrient risk with GLP-1 receptor and dual incretin agonists in obesity: Mechanistic pathways, clinical signals, and a monitoring framework](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2026.100290). A patent on a GLP-1 secretion-promoting nutritional composition (fermented milk, fibre, oligosaccharide) has been on file since 2019 [Glp-1 secretion-promoting nutritional composition](https://patents.google.com/patent/TW201932026A).
- **Time horizon:** Product development and EU launch within 12 months of the board decision, timed to the expanding GLP-1 user base as reimbursement expands in France and the UK.
- **Effort:** No primary source reports the cost of launching a protein-focused food line in Europe; a GLP-1 pharma start-up raised $400 million in 2024 [GLP-1 start-up launches with $400 million - C&EN](https://cen.acs.org/business/start-ups/GLP-1-startlaunches-400-million/102/i31), which is not transferable to a food-company P&L. The effort cannot be sized from this evidence.
- **Risk:** "GLP-1-friendly" is not an authorised claim in the EU [EU register of health claims - Food Safety - European Commission](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims/eu-register-health-claims_en)[Health claims according to Article 13 of the EC Regulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2606982/); marketing must rely on nutrient-content claims ("high in protein," "reduced energy") rather than a GLP-1 reference. Consumer willingness-to-pay in European markets is unmeasured.
- **Against it:** No RCT evidence exists that a specific food product enhances GLP-1 therapy; entering without clinical data risks the line being read as a marketing ploy rather than a therapeutic complement.

### Option 2 — Partner with a precision-nutrition or digital-health platform (Nutrition + Health technology)

- **Trigger:** Kailera launched with $400 million in October 2024 [GLP-1 start-up launches with $400 million - C&EN](https://cen.acs.org/business/start-ups/GLP-1-startlaunches-400-million/102/i31), and Horizon Europe's €95.5 billion (2021–2027) and proposed €175 billion (2028–2034) programmes [Funding programmes and open calls - Research and innovation](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/funding/funding-opportunities/funding-programmes-and-open-calls_en)[Horizon Europe - Research and innovation - European Commission](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/funding/funding-opportunities/funding-programmes-and-open-calls/horizon-europe_en) offer public co-funding for metabolic-health consortia. The 2026 micronutrient-risk paper [Micronutrient risk with GLP-1 receptor and dual incretin agonists in obesity: Mechanistic pathways, clinical signals, and a monitoring framework](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2026.100290) identifies monitoring needs that a digital platform could address.
- **Time horizon:** Partnership agreement signed within 6 months; first co-developed digital-nutrition module live within 12 months.
- **Effort:** A comparable GLP-1-adjacent start-up raised $400 million in 2024 [GLP-1 start-up launches with $400 million - C&EN](https://cen.acs.org/business/start-ups/GLP-1-startlaunches-400-million/102/i31); a European food company partnering with such a platform would likely invest a fraction of that in integration and co-branding, but no primary source sizes this.
- **Risk:** Platform data and IP may be locked; the food company risks becoming a white-label ingredient supplier. The 2026 patent cliff in non-EU markets [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12) may compress pharma margins and reduce their appetite for food-partnership investment.
- **Against it:** The EU has no established reimbursement pathway for digital-nutrition services bundled with GLP-1 therapy; NICE approved Saxenda (December 2020) and Wegovy (March 2023) as drugs, not as therapy-plus-nutrition bundles [Time to unshackle the medical treatment of obesity in the NHS - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11091500/).

### Option 3 — Build an EU health-claims regulatory dossier (Food + Nutrition)

- **Trigger: ** Regulation, EC) 1924/2006, first opinions June 2012) [Health claims (art. 13) - EFSA - Europa.eu](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/health-claims-art-13) define the pathway, the EU register [EU register of health claims - Food Safety - European Commission](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims/eu-register-health-claims_en) currently has no GLP-1 or incretin-related claim. The 2026 nutrition framework [Nutrition-First Support for GLP-1 and Dual Incretin Therapy in Obesity: A Practical Framework for Dietary Management, Symptom Tolerability, and Long-Term Weight Maintenance](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18111751) provides the scientific basis for a structure/function claim (e.g., "contributes to a feeling of fullness") that avoids disease-related wording.
- **Time horizon:** EFSA opinion and Commission decision typically take 18–36 months; file a dossier in 2026 to be in position for 2028–2029 authorisation.
- **Effort:** No primary source reports the cost of an EFSA health-claims dossier; the pathway is regulatory rather than R&D, but the scientific-evidence requirement ("a sufficient number of well-designed studies" [Health claims according to Article 13 of the EC Regulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2606982/)) implies trial investment that cannot be sized from this evidence.
- **Risk:** If the claim is refused, the investment is sunk and US competitors (no pre-market claim authorisation) can use "GLP-1-friendly" freely. The regulatory pathway for a GLP-1-specific food claim is uncertain.
- **Against it:** With no RCTs in the evidence base, the scientific dossier is weak; a structure/function claim is materially weaker marketing than a GLP-1 reference and may not differentiate the product.

The evidence does not quantify volume at risk or cannibalisation for a mid-sized European food company. €12,748.6 five-year cost differential for semaglutide versus placebo in Germany (2025 €) [Cost-Effectiveness and Budget-Impact Analysis of Semaglutide in Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction and Obesity in the German Health-Care System - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13499734/) frame pharma economics but do not translate to food-category volume shifts.
