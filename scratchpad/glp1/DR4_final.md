## What the evidence does not support

- The claim that "GLP-1 friendly" foods can be freely marketed in the EU is contradicted by the regulatory record: L13 and T900000012 confirm such wording requires EFSA health-claim substantiation under Regulation 1924/2006, while T42806 shows majors marketing adjacent products — the two readings coexist, but the EU register governs [How 'GLP-1 friendly' claims are regulated in the UK and EU](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/07/glp-1-friendly-claims-how-theyre-regulated/), [Nutrition and Health Claims - Food Safety - European Commission](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims_en).
- The claim that the GLP-1 drug market is in structural decline or stagnation is refuted by Novo Nordisk's $26B 2024 revenue from Wegovy and Ozempic and the FDA's approval of Foundayo under the accelerated CNPV pathway [Looming GLP-1 drug patent expirations draw generics firms](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/Looming-GLP-1-drug-patent/103/web/2025/12), [FDA Approves First New Molecular Entity Under National Priority Voucher Program — FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-new-molecular-entity-under-national-priority-voucher-program).
- The claim that a mid-sized European company can enter GLP-1 drug development profitably is unsupported: the evidence shows a pharma game dominated by two firms, with Kailera debuting at $400M and Pfizer acquiring Metsera, not an open SME entry [GLP-1 start-up launches with $400 million - C&EN](https://cen.acs.org/business/start-ups/GLP-1-startlaunches-400-million/102/i31), [Pfizer to Acquire Metsera and its Next-Generation Obesity Portfolio — Pfizer](https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizer-acquire-metsera-and-its-next-generation-obesity).
- The claim that European GLP-1 adoption matches the US is contradicted: UK penetration is ~7% in early 2026 versus higher US rates, though adoption is rising [GLP-1s in Europe: Adoption lags behind the US, but adoption is increasing](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/03/27/glp-1s-in-europe-adoption-lags-behind-the-us-but-adoption-is-increasing/), [Will GLP-1s go big in Europe in 2026?](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/01/05/glp-1s-europe-success-in-2026/).

## Options for a mid-sized European company

### Option 1 — Launch a differentiated "companion nutrition" line (protein, fibre, micronutrients)
- Trigger: The documented lean-mass loss of 25–40% of total weight on GLP-1 therapy [Preventing Lean Mass Loss on GLP-1 Therapy: 2026 Evidence Update — Clinical Nutrition Report](https://www.clinicalnutritionreport.com/articles/preventing-lean-mass-loss-glp1/) and the systematic scoping review of nutritional interventions during semaglutide or tirzepatide therapy published April 2025 [Nutrition Strategies for Next‐Generation Incretin Therapies: A Systematic Scoping Review of the Current Evidence - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13136787/).
- Time horizon: Product on shelf within 12 months, before major food companies consolidate the GLP-1 nutrition shelf space.
- Effort: A high-protein meal-replacement launch of this kind is anchored by the Lactalis acquisition of Protein Works (UK) in 2026 as a comparable case for a category leader; a mid-sized entrant should budget a fraction of that for R&D, regulatory substantiation and launch [Dairy majors turn to meal replacements as GLP‑1 reshapes nutrition](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/06/11/dairy-majors-turn-to-meal-replacements-as-glp1-reshapes-nutrition/) (secondary source only).
- Risk: Cannibalisation by Hims & Hers GLP-1 bars and shakes (launched November 2024) and Lactalis/Protein Works; regulatory exposure if "GLP-1 friendly" claims are used without EFSA authorisation [Hims & Hers Add Bars & Shakes for GLP-1 Users](https://athletechnews.com/hims-hers-add-bars-shakes-for-glp-1-users/), [How 'GLP-1 friendly' claims are regulated in the UK and EU](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/07/glp-1-friendly-claims-how-theyre-regulated/) (secondary source only).
- Against it: The EU claims register means any drug-adjacent claim requires EFSA substantiation under Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006, a process that a mid-sized firm may not absorb [Nutrition and Health Claims - Food Safety - European Commission](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims_en), [Health claims (art. 13) - EFSA - Europa.eu](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/health-claims-art-13).

### Option 2 — Partner with a digital-health platform (Hims & Hers, Weight Watchers Med+)
- Trigger: Weight Watchers launched its GLP-1 Med+ program globally on 26 December 2025 with nutrition plans to preserve muscle mass [Weight Watchers 2026: Everything to Know About the New GLP-1 Med+ Program and App](https://hitconsultant.net/2025/12/17/weight-watchers-launches-new-glp-1-program-and-ai-app-features/), and Hims & Hers already sells GLP-1 bars and shakes (November 2024) [Hims & Hers Add Bars & Shakes for GLP-1 Users](https://athletechnews.com/hims-hers-add-bars-shakes-for-glp-1-users/) (secondary source only).

- Effort: Anchored by the eMed $200M Series A at $2B+ valuation (March 2026) as a comparable for a digital GLP-1 platform; a food SME partnering in would need a co-development budget for customised SKUs [eMed Secures $200M Series A at $2B+ Valuation to Scale AI-Powered GLP-1 Employer Platform](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/03/26/emed-200m-series-a-linda-yaccarino-tom-brady-glp-1-employer-costs/) (secondary source only).

- Against it: The partner controls the consumer relationship and the "GLP-1" association, leaving the food company as a supplier with limited equity in the category.

### Option 3 — Apply for EIC Accelerator / Horizon Europe funding to de-risk R&D
- Trigger: The 2026 EIC Work Programme is open, with the EIC Accelerator Open budget at €414M [EIC Accelerator - European Innovation Council - European Commission](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en).
